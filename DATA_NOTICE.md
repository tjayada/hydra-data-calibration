# Data provenance notice

The raw dataset downloaded by `download_data.py` (`24_12_19_hydra_icp_eval.zip`,
658 MB) was created by the authors of the Hydra paper:

> Martin Huber, Huanyu Tian, Christopher E. Mower, Lucas-Raphael Mueller,
> Sebastien Ourselin, Christos Bergeles, Tom Vercauteren.
> "Hydra: Marker-Free RGB-D Hand-Eye Calibration." 2025.
> arXiv:2504.20584. King's College London.

## Status of the data

- The paper's abstract states the benchmarking dataset and code would be
  open-sourced under the Apache 2.0 License. As of this repository's release,
  the dataset itself has **not** been formally published by the authors, and
  no license file accompanies the data.
- The data was shared with the maintainer of this repository by Martin Huber
  (martin.huber@kcl.ac.uk), one of the Hydra authors, who granted access and
  permission to redistribute it for research purposes.
- **All rights to the data remain with Martin Huber and the Hydra authors**
  until they publish it under their chosen license. This repository makes no
  license claim over the data; the MIT license here covers the code only.
